# Community code of conduct

We build high-trust release infrastructure and expect high-trust collaboration.

## Our standard

Be accurate, respectful, and evidence-led. Critique code and claims, not people. Protect private
information. Make room for questions, explain tradeoffs, and correct mistakes plainly. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, impersonation, deliberate disruption, and secret disclosure are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `conduct@echo-op.com`. Include links or screenshots when safe,
but do not forward credentials, customer data, or restricted evidence. Maintainers may edit, hide,
lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its
community. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project spaces, and other forums where a
participant represents the project.
